Questions

Good to know.

Do we have to replace our register?

No. Keep the register you use today and pay 0% in card processing to us. Complete adds sync with your current system, so in-store sales take items off the website.

What do card payments cost on the Tabella reader?

Card processing on the Tabella reader is priced to match the in-person rate of the popular small-business card readers, so no mall pays more to switch. Whether to pass card costs to shoppers is your choice, within card-network rules and your state's law.

What does the mall earn from online sales?

4% of every online sale of an item in one of your booths. Buyers who choose mall pickup come to your counter to collect it.

Is there a contract?

The first 6 months are free on any plan. We'll talk through terms with each founding mall before billing starts.

What if our mall is already on the map?

Your listing is free either way. Write to contact@tabellasuite.com to claim it or correct it.
