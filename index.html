For dealers

Free to join. List it once, sell it everywhere.

Whether you rent a booth or sell on your own, Tabella puts your inventory in front of buyers far beyond the mall's front door, and you get paid in days.

No listing fees and no monthly fee. A 9% commission on online sales only. When your item sits in a Tabella mall, 4% of that goes to the mall.

  • Snap a photo and get a draft listing: description, category, a price range and a rarity badge
  • Print scannable price tags, so every sale at the register credits the right booth
  • A sale anywhere takes the item off everywhere, so nothing sells twice
  • Run your own booth sales, live at the register the moment you post them
  • Shipping estimates by box size, and buyers see tracking
  • A booth finder that matches dealers with malls that have space opening up
