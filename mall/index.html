            Your terms

            Set each booth's monthly rent on the Dealers tab. Card sales pay the month's rent first, then the dealer gets the rest. Any rent still unpaid comes off what you owe on the statement.

            Commission and the card fee come out of each dealer's share. You can set a different commission for one booth on the Dealers tab. Use your combined local rate for sales tax; you file it under your own sales tax license.

            Street address

            Used to register your card reader, and shown to shoppers who buy online for pickup at your counter. Your dealers' pieces can be picked up here once it's filled in.

            Card reader

            Stripe's simulated reader. It works like the real one, with test cards and no money.

            Get paid

            Your commission, card fees and the sales tax from card sales go to your bank through Stripe.
