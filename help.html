For dealers

Open your shop and get paid

Free to join, no monthly fee. Tabella keeps 9% of online sales only. In-store sales are between you and your mall.

  1. Create your shop

    Go to tabellasuite.com/dealer, tap Create an account, and enter your shop name, email and a password. The shop name is what shoppers see.

  2. Add a piece

    Tap Add an item, take a photo, and give it a title and price. Add a few words about condition: wear, chips or repairs.

    To sell it online too, add its box size, and set your ship-from address and phone once. Those only go on shipping labels; shoppers never see them.

  3. Set up payouts

    In the setup box, find Get paid and tap Set up to finish Stripe's setup. You connect a bank account to receive money; you never put in a card or pay anything.

    • Just you: choose individual, and use your own name and bank.
    • You have an LLC or business: choose company, enter the business's legal name and EIN, and use the business bank account. Stripe still asks for your details as the owner.

    The name on the bank account should match the name on the Stripe account. A mismatch is the most common reason payouts get held.

  4. Print tags

    In Inventory, tap Print tags, pick your pieces, and print on a 4 x 6 label (8 tags to cut apart), a 2.25 x 1.25 label roll, or a letter sheet of 24. Each tag has the price, item number, your booth and a code the mall's register scans.

  5. Join your mall

    If your booth is in a mall that uses Tabella, get its join code from the office. In the dealer app, open Your mall, tap Join, and enter the code and your booth number. The mall can then ring up your tagged pieces, and they come off the online shop the moment they sell.

  6. When something sells online

    You get an email. Open the Sales tab and tap Print shipping label (the buyer already paid for it), pack the piece well and ship it within 3 business days. Your share goes to your bank through Stripe.

  7. Looking for a booth?

    Send a mall your shop link: tabellasuite.com/shop/?dealer= followed by your shop name, for example tabellasuite.com/shop/?dealer=shantiques. It shows only your pieces that are set to sell online. Your exact link is in the Your mall step of the dealer app.

For malls

Set up your mall and register

A register that sells your dealers' pieces, reports for each day and month, and a statement for every booth. Runs in the browser on a tablet, phone or computer.

  1. Create your mall account

    Go to tabellasuite.com/mall and tap Create a mall account. Use a different email from any dealer account.

  2. Settings

    Enter your sales tax rate (your combined local rate), your commission and the card fee you charge dealers on card sales, and your time zone. Choose whether to take booth rent out of card payouts.

    Add your street address (only used to register your card reader, never shown), then set up your card reader: Stripe's WisePOS E or S700. Type the code it shows. Last, tap Get paid and connect the mall's bank account.

  3. Bring in your dealers

    On the Dealers tab you'll find your join code. Give it to dealers who use Tabella. For dealers who don't, tap Add a booth: booth number, name, and an email for statements. Set each booth's monthly rent here too.

  4. Ring up a sale

    On the Register, scan a tag with a Bluetooth scanner or the Camera button, or type the item number. For a paper tag or a dealer not on Tabella, open Ring up by booth number and enter the booth and price. Tap Cash or Card, then print the receipt. Pieces come off the online shop the moment they sell.

  5. See the day and the month

    Today and This month show what came in by cash and card, the sales tax, your commission, and what you owe each booth, with a line explaining why. Download a spreadsheet for your books.

  6. Send statements

    On Statements, every booth gets its month: each sale, commission, card fees and rent. Tap Email all statements, or have them go out automatically on the day you pick. Tap Mark paid when you write a dealer's check.

  7. Refunds

    On Today or This month, tap Refund on the sale. Card refunds go back to the card; for cash, hand back the cash. Either way, the pieces go back on sale.

Common questions

Good to know

Do dealers need to put a card into Stripe?

No. Dealers and malls only connect a bank account to receive money. Shoppers' cards pay Tabella, and shipping labels come out of the shipping the buyer paid.

Who handles sales tax?

Tabella collects and pays sales tax on online sales. In the mall, the mall collects it at the register at its local rate and files it under its own license.

When does card money reach a dealer?

Right after the sale, Tabella sends the dealer's share to their Stripe account, and it reaches their bank on Stripe's normal payout schedule. If a dealer hasn't connected a bank yet, the money waits and goes out as soon as they do.

Can we keep the register we already have?

Yes. Tabella can run alongside it. Mark pieces sold in Tabella so they come off the online shop.

Where do I get help?

Write to contact@tabellasuite.com. We read every message.
