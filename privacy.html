Privacy

Privacy, in plain words.

Last updated November 18, 2026. This covers tabellasuite.com as it is today: a website with a sign-up form and a mall map. We will update it before accounts, buying or the scanner app open.

What we collect

Only what you type into the early-access form: your name, email, the kind of user you are, and optionally your mall or booth and the software you use. We have no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies on this site.

Why

To write back to you about early access. We don't sell your details, and we don't share them with anyone except the services that run the form and our email.

Who handles it

The form is delivered to us by Formspree. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the map draws from OpenStreetMap. Those services may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, under their own privacy policies.

Shops on the mall map

The map lists public business details: the shop's name and town, plus a short note on what it is best for from our own visit. Pins are placed by town, not street address. If you run a listed shop and want to claim, correct or remove your listing, write to us and we will act on it.

Removing your details

Email contact@tabellasuite.com and we will delete what you sent us.

Coming later

When shopper accounts and the scanner open, we will say plainly at sign-up what is saved (for example, items you scan and put back, so we can tell you when they are marked down), and you will be able to delete that history at any time.
