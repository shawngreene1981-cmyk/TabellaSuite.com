Miss Lucille's Marketplace

Featured on a Tabella Preferred Buyer Road TripUnclaimed

More than 200 booths, all well kept and beautifully styled, with a cafe in the middle. This is a place to wander. One dealer, JW Mercantile, has booths all over the store, and spotting them became a game.

From our visit, Sat, Nov 14
  • Best for: hours of browsing, perusing and hunting
  • Know before you go: restroom on site; no haggling; little to no cell signal inside
  • We brought home: a jadeite hen on nest, a brass bucket, a Strawberry Shortcake Caboodle, a metal wall hanger that will become a stained glass piece, and a handful of Pokémon cards
  • Tip: plan a few hours and have lunch at the cafe.
  • On Tabella coming soon: search the whole mall from your phone before you go, so the jadeite is the first stop, not the last.
